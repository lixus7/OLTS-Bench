Section 2 · How to read the numbers

Gain = (frozen error - online error) / frozen error. Positive means the online model is better: +20% means 20% lower error than the frozen model; -20% means 20% higher error; -250% means 3.5 times the error. Gains are at most +100% and unbounded below, so every table uses medians.

A pair = two TEST runs with the same dataset, backbone, split, horizon H and seed, each scored by MSE on the same forecast origins; it gives one gain.

Example: ETTh1 · DLinear · O split · H = 96 · seed 2024 · ER: online W96 has MSE 0.9988, frozen W512 has 0.9523, so gain = (0.9523 − 0.9988) / 0.9523 = −4.9%.

Each cell shows the median; the small "k/n" says in how many of the n pairs the online model was better. Blue: online better; orange: frozen better; grey: within +-5%.
